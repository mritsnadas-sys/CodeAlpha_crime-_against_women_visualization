Crime Against Women in India — Data Visualization

-Project Overview:
This project focuses on creating meaningful data visualizations from crime against women data in India.
The purpose of this project is to transform crime statistics into clear and easy-to-understand visual representations. The visualizations help identify patterns, trends, comparisons, and changes in recorded crime cases across different years and states.
The analysis covers the period 2019 to 2024.

-Objectives:
The main objectives of this visualization project are:
Visualize crime trends across different years.
Compare different crime categories.
Analyze state-wise crime patterns.
Identify significant increases and decreases in recorded cases.
Present complex crime data in a simple and understandable form.
Use charts and graphs to communicate important observations effectively.

-Dataset:
The dataset contains recorded crime against women cases across Indian states and Union Territories.

-Visualizations:
The project contains multiple visualizations to understand the data from different perspectives.
1. Year-wise Crime Trend
A year-wise visualization is used to observe how recorded crime cases changed between 2019 and 2024.
This helps identify crime categories that increased, decreased, or fluctuated over time.

2. Crime Category Comparison
A comparison visualization is used to understand which crime categories had relatively higher or lower numbers of recorded cases.
This makes it easier to compare the magnitude of different crime categories.

3. State-wise Crime Visualization
State-level visualizations are used to compare recorded crime cases across different states and Union Territories.

This helps identify states with comparatively higher and lower recorded cases in the dataset.

-Key Observations:
Some important patterns observed through the visualizations include:
Rape And Murder had the highest number of recorded cases among the analyzed crime categories.
Rape showed a substantial increase from 2019 to 2024.
Sexual Harassment also showed a significant increase during the study period.
Domestic Violence increased over the analyzed years.
Acid Attack showed a strong decline from 2019 to 2024.
Crime patterns varied considerably between different states.
The visualizations show that different crime categories followed different trends rather than changing uniformly.

-wise trends, differences between crime categories, and state-wise variations in recorded crime cases.

Data visualization provides a clearer way to communicate complex datasets and helps viewers understand important patterns without having to examine large tables of numbers.

Note: The visualizations describe patterns in the provided dataset and should not be interpreted as a complete representation of the actual incidence of crime.

Data Limitations:
The visualizations are based on recorded/reported cases in the provided dataset. Therefore:
The data may not represent all actual incidents.
Differences in reporting practices between states may affect comparisons.
The analysis covers only the available period of 2019–2024.
Raw case counts do not account for differences in population between states.
The visualizations show patterns in the data but do not establish the causes behind those patterns.
